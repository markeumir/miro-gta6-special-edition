Chapter 1 — protect your assets BEFORE Recalibration

Finish house jobs before Coming Home. Recalibration can trigger when you return home after Lucia calls; do not assume there will be free roam before Tourist Trap.

  1. Collect pending mailbox payouts and empty the home stash.
  2. Deposit at an ATM, then transfer Checking funds to Family Savings in BuckMe. Aim for the first $5,000 milestone.
  3. Spend surplus on permanent purchases and verify unique weapon ownership.
  4. Keep a separate manual save before returning home for Recalibration. Save again before Tourist Trap only if available.
  5. Defer non-missable large cash rewards, including Hyde Island, until Chapter 2.

Family Savings is not spendable cash. Pocket cash, ordinary Checking, home stash and the original safehouse/car are at risk from scripted losses.

💰 Cash & Family Savings

Chapter 1: Before Recalibration (Talk to Lucia), retrieve floorboard cash, collect outstanding mailbox payouts, contribute at least $5,000 to Family Savings if possible, and spend surplus on permanent weapons and clothing. Recalibration can wipe carried cash; Tourist Trap can wipe cash, ordinary checking, the house stash and access to the original house and car. Do not rely on an ATM alone to protect you against the story reset. Family Savings cannot be withdrawn. Save before Recalibration starts; make another save before Tourist Trap only if the game permits. Defer valuable non-missable cash rewards until Chapter 2 when possible.

Routine play: Before risky jobs, deposit surplus at an ATM once BuckMe is unlocked. Carry only the cash you need; check account capacity and keep Family Savings separate. Banking reduces loss risk, but guides differ about checking deductions after death; do not assume zero loss. Before BuckMe, the home stash can help temporarily, but empty it before the Chapter 1 cutoff.

🔫 Rifles, unique weapons & ammunition

Purchased weapons and properly collected gold/unique variants are reported to be owned permanently. Confirm the named rifle appears in Manage Weapons at an Ammu-Nation locker or personal vehicle trunk; the gold variant should be available even without buying the standard version. Use these menus to retrieve owned weapons after being Wasted or Busted. Ordinary borrowed or picked-up weapons can disappear on death or arrest; the trunk can temporarily store them but is not a permanent ownership guarantee. Purchase an ordinary weapon if you want dependable ownership.

After a rare pickup such as the Island Poacher FF-50, confirm collection at a locker, check ammo, and save in a new slot. Keep the pre-encounter save until the reward is confirmed. Do not assume ammo or attachments on every weapon survive every event.

🎒 Gear, clothing & cargo

Check your owned clothing in the wardrobe and equipment in the loadout. Keep useful spares in an owned vehicle if available, but don't treat a trunk as a vault. Stash bags, carried loot and cargo may be lost on death, arrest, impound or vehicle destruction. Sell or securely unload loot when the game permits; record actual proceeds only after sale. Check the inventory after switching characters, after missions and after recovering a vehicle. Do not assume consumables or gear persist through scripted encounters.

🚗 Rare cars & vehicle recovery

Driving a vehicle is not ownership. After the Moses mission in Chapter 2, scan eligibility and register an eligible untracked vehicle; later story unlocks expand available registrations. Confirm the vehicle is marked personal/registered, check available garage space and store it. Avoid expensive customization of unowned cars or the original Chapter 1 car before the reset. Save after registering a rare vehicle; do not assume aircraft access equals permanent ownership.

If a registered vehicle disappears, check the Personal tab of the in-game map and Scooter Bros delivery/recovery when unlocked. Fees may apply; an impounded or destroyed car may not return with its original cargo or gear. Unregistered cars are not guaranteed recoverable. Verify ownership and inventory after recovery.

💾 Save checkpoints & recovery

Outside missions, PS5 Options → Game → Save Game. Keep separate slots before rare encounters, Recalibration, Tourist Trap and other chapter gates; save again after verifying loot, ownership and inventory. Rotate routine saves instead of overwriting your only good checkpoint. Loading an older manual save can replace the autosave; keep the latest good state in a separate manual slot first. Avoid saving over a pre-exploit slot, and save before enabling cheats (cheat sessions restrict saving).

Optional: if you have PS Plus, check your PS5 saved-data sync status and timestamps before relying on cloud recovery. The companion's JSON backup saves only checklist/notes/session history, not actual GTA VI game progress or assets.
